## Internal cohort baseline

- **Status:** complete
- **Evidence:** [Internal baseline results](../BASELINE-RESULTS.md), [internal cohort figure](../outputs/affinity-distributions.png)

**What and why:** Train three fixed forests on 7,393 training complexes using a training-only 36-feature mask; evaluate the frozen configuration on 2,171 test complexes.

**Result:** Test RMSE was 1.5516 ± 0.0075 p-affinity, equivalent to 2.1167 ± 0.0102 kcal/mol under the project’s 298.15 K conversion; the journal reports 2.10 ± 0.003 kcal/mol. Author settings and conversion are not confirmed, so this is not an exact reproduction claim.

## BDB2020+ evaluation

- **Status:** complete
- **Evidence:** [BDB2020+ evaluation](../BASELINE-RESULTS.md), [BDB2020+ figure](../outputs/bdb2020-predictions.png)

**What and why:** Apply the same three frozen models to 115 BDB2020+ records without refitting or seed selection.

**Result:** RMSE was 1.2088 ± 0.0206 p-affinity, equivalent to 1.6491 ± 0.0282 kcal/mol under the project convention; the journal reports 1.61 ± 0.008 kcal/mol. The cause of the difference is unknown, and similarity and assay provenance remain open.
